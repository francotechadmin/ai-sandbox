# Power failure: response checklist

1. For overload (over 9000 W), reduce feed or speed until power is back under 8500 W.
2. For under-power (below 3500 W), inspect the drive belt or coupling for slip and the motor for a lost phase.
3. Lock out before inspecting mechanical parts.
4. Measure supply voltage at the machine; low voltage causes torque loss.
5. Check the motor and drive for fault codes and overheating.
6. Check the tool; a worn tool raises torque and so power.
7. Restart and record power over a full cycle.
8. If power stays outside the band, call an electrician or the drive supplier.
