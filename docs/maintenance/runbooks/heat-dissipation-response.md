# Heat dissipation failure: response checklist

1. Reduce load or stop the machine if the process temperature is still climbing.
2. Lock out before opening guards or panels.
3. Clear dust and debris from heat exchangers, fans and vents.
4. Replace clogged filters.
5. Verify coolant level and pump operation; top up with the specified coolant only.
6. Check that fan rotation is correct and that the fan reaches full speed.
7. Restart at normal speed and watch the temperature difference for 15 minutes. It should recover above 9 K.
8. If it does not, escalate to a maintenance engineer for a heat exchanger or pump check.
