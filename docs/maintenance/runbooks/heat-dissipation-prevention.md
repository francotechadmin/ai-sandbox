# Heat dissipation failure: prevention

- Clean fans, vents and heat exchangers on a weekly schedule, more often in dusty areas.
- Do not run long cycles at low rotational speed in warm conditions; low speed means less airflow.
- Alert when `process_temp_k - air_temp_k` drops under 9 K, before the 8.6 K failure threshold.
- Keep a record of coolant changes and filter replacements.
- Keep at least a clear working space around the machine for ventilation.
